# Changelog

## 1.0

First release. 265 models: Oak, Birch, Maple, Beech, Pine, Spruce, Fir in five growth stages and
five variants; Bush, BushDense, BushFull, Bramble, Fern in three sizes; Branch, Log, Stump.
Scripts: Setup, Verify, Wind, Seasons with Palettes, BuildChunk with 30 chunk recipes.

Fixed before release, after the first Studio test: the hairline crack between a trunk and its
swaying top (the top now overlaps the trunk from inside), no collision above the lowest branch
(every tree carries an invisible solid Collider tube in the trunk), the birch's dark base (white to
the ground with black bark marks), the wind script costing time while switched off, and the model
pivot sitting mid-trunk instead of at the base.
